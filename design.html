PeoplePulse · Decision Brief

Student Meal Service — Should we pilot a weekly plan?

Project: Campus meals · Audience: University students 18–25 · 1,284 respondents · 12–19 May

The Question

What were we trying to understand? Whether students would consider a ₦2,000/week meal plan.

What We Found

Observed pattern: 64% of respondents would consider it. Price sensitivity clustered around delivery time.

Why People Said It

“It saves cooking time during exams.” Convenience outweighed variety for 3 in 5 open-text explanations.

What To Investigate Next

Test the ₦2,000 price point directly, and ask why the 14% declined.

Evidence limitations: self-selected sample; not representative of all students. Interpretation ≠ prediction. What remains uncertain: repeat purchase intent.
