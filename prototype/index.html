Decision Brief · flagship output (preview)

Should we pilot the weekly plan?

Project: Campus meals · Audience: University students 18–25 · 1,284 respondents

The Question

Whether students would consider a ₦2,000/week plan.

What We Found

Observed pattern: most respondents would consider it; price sensitivity centers on delivery time.

Why People Said It

“It saves cooking time during exams.” Convenience outweighs variety in open-text answers.

What Remains Uncertain

Repeat purchase intent and the 14% who declined — next follow-up.
